
Authenticated action limits implemented on branch: send (direct/group messages, statuses, uploads) share 300 requests/account/minute; contact (friend requests, group creation/member-add, direct/group call start) share 30 requests/account/5 minutes. Counts include invalid, duplicate and retry requests; group member batches count as one request, not one per member. This is a fixed-window request limit, not a global bandwidth or signaling limit. The limiter fails closed if its Durable Object is unavailable; clients can receive 429 and account budgets are shared across devices.
